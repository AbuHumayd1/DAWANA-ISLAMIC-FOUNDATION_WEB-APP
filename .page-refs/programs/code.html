Participation Architecture

How to Participate & Engage

Clear, straightforward access paths to all Dawana programs and learning assemblies.

1

Open Weekly Halāqāt

Physical Usrah, Friday Tadhkirah at Iponri Central Mosque, and alternating Friday night online treatises are open to the entire Muslim community. Attend freely without any prior registration.

door_open Open Admission · Free of Charge
2

Cohort & Specialty Programs

Targeted initiatives like Youth Outreach (including Youth Outreach 2.0) and academic Symposiums require a brief delegate registration when enrollment windows open.

how_to_reg Online Application · Free of Charge
3

Darul Islam Camp (DIC) Intake

Registration for DIC follows an administrative workflow: complete the digital intake form, undergo application review, fulfill required delegate dues, and receive confirmed delegate access.

verified_user Vetting → Payment → Badge Issuance
