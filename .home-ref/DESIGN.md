## Brand & Style

The design system projects an aura of institutional authority, cultural heritage, and contemporary humanitarian excellence. It balances the timeless serenity of Islamic scholarly traditions with an active, forward-facing modern civic initiative.

### Brand Personality & Core Values
- **Dignity & Reverence:** Quietly authoritative without feeling austere; deep, meditative forest tones reflect institutional maturity.
- **Warm Hospitality (Karam):** Welcoming and community-centered; avoiding sterile corporate stiffness through warm alabaster surfaces and luminous gold accents.
- **Clarity & Trust:** Absolute typographic precision and uncluttered layouts evoke transparency, civic responsibility, and fiscal integrity.

### Aesthetic Direction & Visual Style
The design style is **Contemporary Institutional Editorial**. It merges classical proportioned typography with modern, tactile container architectures.
- **Architectural Motifs:** Elegant parabolic and multi-foil arch geometries are reinterpreted as subtle border curves, tab shapes, and framed hero masks.
- **Ornamental Subtlety:** Star polygons and girih line tessellations are deployed strictly at low opacity (3%–5%) as subtle textures over rich spruce or sand surfaces, never as overwhelming decoration.
- **Art Direction for Imagery:** Photography showcases authentic African Muslim life—scholarly circles, civic empowerment programs, communal warmth, and bright daylight—framed cleanly with fine hairline borders.

## Colors

The palette draws deeply from historical botanical inks, polished brass instruments, and warm sandstone courtyards.

### Palette Hierarchy & Application
- **Primary Spruce (`#0F4D38`):** The institutional anchor. Used for high-impact surfaces, mastheads, primary CTA buttons, and dominant brand elements.
- **Secondary Antique Gold (`#C89B3C`):** Represents excellence and spiritual warmth. Reserved for key action accents, active highlights, micro-badges, indicators, and gilded framing lines.
- **Tertiary Deep Forest (`#186047`):** An elevation tone used for hover states on primary components, dark surface layering, and highlighted editorial blocks.
- **Neutral Charcoal (`#2D3734`):** The primary ink for body copy and high-legibility typographic tokens, paired with an ultra-deep foundation (`#1A2421`) for titles.

### Supporting Canvas & Surface Tones
- **Canvas Base (`#FBFBFA`):** An unbleached warm alabaster providing soft reading comfort.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#F5F7F5`):** Subtly tinted container fill for secondary modules and interactive fields.
- **Surface Hairline Border (`#E2E8E4`):** Delivers structural definition across all cards and section partitions.
- **Lustre Trim (`#E5C365` / `#D4AF37`):** Fine decorative outlines and metadata badges.

## Typography

Typography establishes an immediate balance between regal heritage and modern legibility.

### Hierarchy & Editorial Rules
- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for institutional titles, major statements, and article headings. Uses medium and semi-bold weights with gentle negative letter-spacing for tight, dignified display impact.
- **Body & UI Elements (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers ultra-clear legibility across dense administrative texts, project briefs, and interactive forms. Generous line-height preserves readability in editorial passages.
- **Labels & Micro-Accents:** Rendered in uppercase bold weights of `Plus Jakarta Sans` with expanded letter spacing (`0.08em`) to demarcate categories, dates, and campaign statuses.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt modular grid structured to evoke rhythmic balance, symmetry, and generous spatial breathing room.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column fluid grid, max-width container capped at `1280px`, centered with `margin-desktop` (64px) and `gutter-desktop` (32px).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with `24px` gutters and `32px` page margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `gutter-mobile` (16px) and `margin-mobile` (20px). Content stacks vertically with full-bleed cards or bounded margin insets.

### Vertical Rhythm
Layouts alternate between dense informative data sets and open, contemplative editorial panels. Major narrative sections are separated by `space-3xl` (96px) on desktop and `space-xl` (48px) on mobile, giving proper prominence to key communal programs.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation eschews harsh synthetic drop shadows in favor of ambient warmth and crisp architectural borders.

### Surface Tiers & Depth Metaphors
- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** The base canvas (`#FBFBFA`) uses structural hairlines (`1px solid #E2E8E4`) without shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Soft ambient occlusion tinted with spruce tone: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 77, 56, 0.05), 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)` with a `1px solid #E2E8E4` border.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Float):** Elevated interactive element: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(15, 77, 56, 0.09), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 77, 56, 0.03)` with gold transition (`border-color: #C89B3C`).
- **Level 3 (Modals & Sticky Panels):** Institutional floating overlay: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(11, 60, 44, 0.16)`. Backdrop utilizes a frosted blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(251, 251, 250, 0.85)`).

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses standard rounded corners (`0.5rem` / 8px base) expanding up to soft architectural curves (`1rem` to `1.5rem` / 16px–24px) for prominent content containers.

### Corner Radius System
- **Compact Controls (`rounded` / 8px):** Input fields, inline selects, list cells.
- **Cards & Modules (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Program cards, donation panels, and testimonial frames.
- **Hero Windows & Architectural Panels (`rounded-xl` / 24px):** Major featured containers. Top borders may occasionally incorporate bespoke subtle arch curvature.
- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** Filter tags, status indicators, and currency chips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in spruce (`#0F4D38`), foreground white (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded` (8px), padding `12px 24px`. Hover state deepens to `#186047` with a fine amber focus ring (`2px solid #C89B3C`).
- **Secondary / Gilded:** Background transparent, `1.5px solid #C89B3C`, text `#0F4D38`. Hover state fills with `#C89B3C` at 10% opacity.
- **Tertiary (Text):** Text `#0F4D38`, font weight 600, paired with an animated directional chevron.

### Cards
- Standard cards feature a pure white or warm alabaster background, wrapped in a 1px `#E2E8E4` border and `16px` border-radius.
- Cards host a subtle geometric tessellation pattern (3% opacity) in the top header zone.
- Cause and donation cards include a gilded progress meter (`#C89B3C`) on an emerald trace channel (`#EAEFEA`).

### Input Fields
- Built with crisp `1px solid #E2E8E4` borders, `8px` corner radius, and subtle warm white fills (`#FFFFFF`).
- Focus state shifts the border to `#0F4D38` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(15, 77, 56, 0.1)`).
- Error states swap border and text cues to `#9E2A2B`.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped status indicators with a soft emerald background (`#EAEFEA`), deep spruce text (`#0F4D38`), and uppercase `label-sm` tracking.
- Highlight badges feature gold-tinted fills (`rgba(200, 155, 60, 0.12)`) and antique gold typography (`#946E19`).

### Islamic Motif Dividers & Arch Headers
- Section dividers employ a central geometric star point flanked by tapered hairline strokes (`#E2E8E4`).
- Featured imagery containers can utilize top-arch masking (`border-radius: 40px 40px 16px 16px`) echoing classical architectural symmetry.
